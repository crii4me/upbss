How it works

Our four steps to selling your property

Most sellers are surprised by how little of this lands on them. Here is the division of labour.

We appraise it properly

A consultant visits, measures, and prices against comparable sales that actually completed — then shows you those comparables.

We market it well

Professional photography, a floor plan, and placement on every major portal plus our own registered buyer list.

We handle the traffic

Every enquiry is qualified, every viewing is followed up, and you get the feedback unedited — including the awkward parts.

We get it over the line

We negotiate the offer, check the buyer’s funding, and keep the conveyancing moving until the keys change hands.

Our fee

There is no agency fee until the property is sold.

When your property completes, our fee falls due — either a fixed amount or a percentage of the sale price. Which one, and how much, is written on your proposal before you sign anything. There are no listing charges and no monthly retainer.

  • No upfront listing charge on our standard agreement
  • Photography, floor plan and portal placement included
  • Fee and minimum term stated on page one of the proposal
  • Free re-valuation at any point while you are on our books
  • Conveyancing and legal costs are separate — and we say so up front

Why sell with UPBSS

What you actually get

A named consultant

One person owns your sale end to end. You get their direct line and their mobile, not a ticket number.

Evidence-based pricing

We show you the completed comparable sales behind the figure. If you disagree with the number, you can argue with the evidence.

Every major portal

Your listing goes to the national portals and to our own registered buyer list on the same day it goes live.

Buyers who are vetted

We ask about position and proof of funds before an offer reaches you, so you are choosing between real buyers.

Feedback within 24 hours

Viewing feedback reaches you unedited the next working day — including the parts you would rather not read.

Chased to completion

We stay on the solicitors, the lender and the buyer until the title is registered with Registers of Scotland.
