Accessibility Policy

How we try to make upbss usable for everyone, and what to do if we have missed something.

Draft template. This policy is written as a working starting point for the upbss build. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and check it against your actual data processing, before the site goes live.

On this page

  • The standard we aim for
  • What is in place
  • Known limitations
  • Contact us about accessibility

Last updated 1 July 2026

The standard we aim for

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Accessibility is treated as part of building a page, not as a later audit.

What is in place

  • Text and background colours meet the AA contrast minimum in both the light and dark themes
  • Every interactive control is reachable and operable with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring
  • A skip link jumps straight to the main content
  • Form fields have permanent visible labels; errors appear next to the field they belong to
  • Icons are decorative and hidden from assistive technology; icon-only buttons carry an accessible name
  • Animation is reduced or removed when your system requests reduced motion
  • Headings follow a logical order and the page works at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling

Known limitations

The property artwork is generated illustration rather than photography and is marked decorative, so screen readers skip it — the written description carries the same information. If you find something that does not work with your assistive technology, please tell us; that report is the most useful thing we can get.

Contact us about accessibility

Email hello@upbss.com with the page address and what went wrong. We aim to respond within five working days and to fix confirmed issues in the next release.
