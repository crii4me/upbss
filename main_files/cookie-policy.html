Cookie Policy

What we store on your device, why, and how to turn it off.

Draft template. This policy is written as a working starting point for the upbss build. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and check it against your actual data processing, before the site goes live.

On this page

  • What cookies we use
  • Analytics
  • Third-party requests
  • Controlling storage

Last updated 1 July 2026

What cookies we use

This build of the site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. We use browser local storage for one thing only:

  • upbss-theme — remembers whether you chose the light or dark version of the site. It contains no personal data and is never sent to a server.

Analytics

If analytics are added to the live site, this page will be updated to name the provider, state what is collected, and give you a working opt-out before any such cookie is set.

Third-party requests

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. That request reveals your IP address to Google. If you would rather it did not, self-host the font files and remove the Google Fonts link from the page head — the site is designed to fall back gracefully to system fonts.

Controlling storage

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time from your browser settings. Clearing the theme entry simply means the site follows your operating system’s light or dark setting again.
