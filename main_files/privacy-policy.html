Privacy Policy

How upbss collects, uses and protects the personal data you give us.

Draft template. This policy is written as a working starting point for the upbss build. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and check it against your actual data processing, before the site goes live.

On this page

  • Who we are
  • What we collect
  • Why we use it
  • Who we share it with
  • How long we keep it
  • Your rights
  • Security

Last updated 1 July 2026

Who we are

upbss Property Group, Landstraße 47, 4020 Linz, Austria, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach our data protection contact at hello@upbss.com.

What we collect

We collect only what we need in order to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to market or purchase a property.

  • Contact details you submit — name, email address, telephone number
  • Enquiry content — what you asked us and about which property
  • Property details you enter into the appraisal tool — type, location, area, age and condition
  • Where required by law, identity and source-of-funds documentation
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and pages visited, in aggregate

Why we use it

We process your data to respond to enquiries and provide the services you asked for (contract), to meet legal obligations such as anti-money-laundering checks (legal obligation), and to improve the site (legitimate interests). Marketing emails are sent only where you have opted in, and every one carries an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We share data only where it is necessary: with the property owner or prospective buyer relevant to your enquiry, with notaries and lenders you have asked us to work with, with the portals on which a listing appears, and with our IT and hosting providers under written processing agreements. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a transaction are deleted after 24 months. Transaction records are kept for the statutory retention period applicable in Austria, currently seven years. Appraisal submissions are deleted after 24 months unless you become a client.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise any of these, email us and we will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna.

Security

Data is transmitted over TLS and stored on access-controlled systems. Access is limited to staff who need it for their role. In the event of a breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and the supervisory authority as required by law.

Questions about this document? Email hello@upbss.com or write to upbss Property Group, Landstraße 47, 4020 Linz, Austria.
