Complaints Procedure

If we get something wrong, here is exactly how we put it right and by when.

Draft template. This policy is written as a working starting point for the upbss build. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and check it against your actual data processing, before the site goes live.

On this page

  • Step 1 — tell us
  • Step 2 — formal complaint

Last updated 1 July 2026

Step 1 — tell us

Raise it first with the consultant handling your sale or purchase. Most problems are a misunderstanding and get resolved the same day. If you would rather not, go straight to step 2.

Step 2 — formal complaint

Write to hello@upbss.com marking it ‘Formal complaint’, or post it to upbss Property Group, Landstraße 47, 4020 Linz. Please include:

  • Your name, address and a daytime telephone number
  • The property address or reference number
  • What happened, with dates where you have them
  • What you would like us to do about it

Our timescales

We acknowledge every formal complaint within three working days. A director not previously involved investigates and sends you a written response within fifteen working days. If the matter is complex and we need longer, we will tell you why and give you a date.

If you are still not satisfied

You may refer the matter to the relevant Austrian professional body for real estate agents, or to the ordinary courts. Nothing in this procedure limits your statutory rights, and using it does not affect your right to take legal action.

Questions about this document? Email hello@upbss.com or write to upbss Property Group, Landstraße 47, 4020 Linz, Austria.
